PII masking (PII means personally identifiable information), and it’s a good feature to show in a RAG portfolio. 
The idea is to replace private details with placeholders before any text is sent to the LLM. 
The model then never sees them, so it can’t repeat, store or learn them.

Images in pdf: Admin uploads a PDF. As now, the text is extracted. In addition, the app pulls out every image using PyMuPDF, a free PDF library.
Small images are skipped (logos, icons, lines), so you don’t pay for describing decorations.
Each remaining image is described once by a vision model, at upload time, not on every question. Online, Groq lists qwen/qwen3.8-27b as a vision model.
